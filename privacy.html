Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Porch Talk Vex Uploader (“the app”) handles information. The app is a personal tool used only by its owner, Jeremy Wise, to manage the Porch Talk Vex YouTube channel.

Who this app is for

The app is not a public service. It is used only by its owner. It does not have other user accounts and is not offered to the public for sign-in or use.

What the app does

The app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • Upload videos to the owner’s own YouTube channel
  • Read statistics and comments from that same channel

Information the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app may access YouTube data for the owner’s channel, such as video metadata, upload data, public channel statistics, and comments on the owner’s videos.

How information is used and stored

  • The app uses this information only to operate the owner’s channel: uploading videos and reviewing that channel’s statistics and comments.
  • Access tokens are stored locally on the owner’s computer. They are not uploaded to this website or to any third-party service other than Google, as needed to talk to the YouTube Data API.
  • This website is a static information site. It does not collect personal information, use cookies or tracking, or run analytics scripts.

What the app does not do

The app does not:

  • Collect, sell, or share data from other users
  • Use Google user data for advertising
  • Transfer Google user data to third parties except as needed to provide the app’s functionality through Google’s own APIs, or as required by law
  • Allow people other than the owner to access the connected YouTube account

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to revoke access

The owner can revoke the app’s access to their Google Account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is revoked, the app can no longer use the YouTube Data API with that account until the owner signs in again.
